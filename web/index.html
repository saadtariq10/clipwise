How it works

From a long video to ready-to-post clips

Four steps, fully automatic. You review, tweak and download.

Paste or upload

A YouTube video, a live stream, or a file from your computer.

Read the audience

Chat spikes, timestamped comments, most-replayed parts and audio energy.

Judge the content

Hook, emotion, payoff. Then AI re-ranks the best and writes titles.

Cut & caption

Vertical or wide clips with word-by-word captions, SRT and transcript.

Features

Everything a clipping team does, in one app

Built for creators and editors who'd rather post than scrub through hours of footage.

Audience-driven picks

Moments viewers actually reacted to: chat bursts, comments like “8:14 😭”, and YouTube's most-replayed data.

Live monitor

Clip a stream while it's on air. Clipwise checks for new moments every few minutes until it ends.

AI review & titles

Gemini or Groq re-rank candidates and write titles, captions and hashtags, with automatic fallbacks.

Music-proof captions

Strips background music from the voice before transcribing, so narration over music stays accurate.

Captions that pop

Word-by-word highlighted captions burned in, plus SRT files and full transcripts for every clip.

Built-in editor

Drag a clip's start and end, change the format or captions, and re-render in seconds.

What's checked

LicenseCreative Commons vs standard YouTube license
MusicSongs YouTube identified in the video

Your own video file

MusicFinds every part with background music, second by second
SongsNames each song (Shazam), after removing the voices
WatermarksTikTok / Instagram marks, @creator tags, fixed logos
BroadcastsTV, sports and studio logos, live scoreboards, movie framing
Web searchOptional: finds your frames online (Google Cloud Vision key)
FixOne click: remove the music, keep the voice

Only short music snippets are sent to Shazam (and a few frames to Google, if you add a key); everything else runs on this computer. YouTube's Content ID database is private, so for a guaranteed answer upload as Private and check YouTube Studio's Checks step. Not legal advice.
